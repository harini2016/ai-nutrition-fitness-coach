# Lifestyle-Only Clinical Nutrition Guidelines (Public Reference)

## Type 2 Diabetes & Insulin Resistance
- Target low-glycemic index foods (glycemic load < 10 per meal).
- Pair complex carbohydrates with lean protein and healthy fats to blunt postprandial glucose spikes.
- Avoid refined sugars, white bread, white rice, and sweetened beverages.
- Distribute carbohydrates evenly across meals (typically 35g-55g net carbs per meal depending on daily budget).

## Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS)
- Emphasize high-protein breakfasts (25g-35g protein) and anti-inflammatory whole foods (leafy greens, berries, fatty fish, nuts, seeds).
- Prioritize high-fiber carbohydrates (legumes, non-starchy vegetables) to improve insulin sensitivity.
- Limit ultra-processed foods and high-glycemic snacks.

## Thyroid Health (Hypothyroidism / Hashimoto's)
- Ensure adequate intake of selenium-rich and zinc-rich whole foods (e.g., eggs, legumes, seeds, fish).
- Maintain consistent meal timing and balanced macronutrient distribution to support metabolic energy.
- Note: Lifestyle guidance only; never adjust or recommend thyroid hormone medications.
